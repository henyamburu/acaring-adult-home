Developmental disability-related inquiries

We welcome inquiries for adults who may benefit from steady routines, respectful reminders, personal care support, supervision, and a smaller home environment. Fit depends on assessment, safety needs, staffing, licensing, specialty designation where applicable, and whether the home can safely meet the resident's needs.

We welcome dementia-related inquiries and review each situation carefully. Fit depends on supervision needs, safety considerations, daily routines, staffing, licensing, specialty designation where applicable, and whether the home is appropriate for the resident.

Daily living support

For adults who need practical help with everyday life in a safe, home-like environment.

Bathing, dressing, and grooming support
Home-style meals and snacks
Medication reminders or assistance
Laundry, housekeeping, and basic routines

A good fit may include adults who need

  • A smaller residential home setting
  • Help with daily routines and reminders
  • Personal care support such as bathing, dressing, grooming, and hygiene
  • Meals, laundry, and household support
  • Medication reminders or assistance according to the care plan
  • Supervision, structure, and respectful daily support
  • Family or representative communication as appropriate

Fit is reviewed individually

Services and placement depend on assessment, resident needs, safety considerations, licensing requirements, specialty designation where applicable, staffing, current availability, payer requirements, and whether the home can safely meet the resident's needs.
