Questions Families Ask

Answer concerns before the first call.

Common questions about availability, referrals, care fit, and visits.

An Adult Family Home is a licensed residential setting that provides care and daily support in a smaller home environment. Services and fit depend on assessment, licensing requirements, staffing, availability, and whether the home can safely meet the resident's needs.

Adults who need a smaller residential care setting, daily supervision, personal care support, medication assistance, meals, structure, and respectful routines may be a good fit. Final fit depends on assessment, care needs, licensing requirements, safety considerations, staffing, and current availability.

Families can call, email, or use the general inquiry form on this page. Please share only basic contact information, your role, the general type of support being requested, and your preferred way to be contacted.
