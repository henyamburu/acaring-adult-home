Federal Way Adult Family Home

Local support for families and referral partners.

ACaring Adult Home provides Adult Family Home support in Federal Way, Washington for adults who need daily help in a smaller residential setting. Families, guardians, case managers, discharge planners, and placement coordinators can contact the home to discuss availability, general care fit, and appropriate next steps.

Start with a general conversation

Please keep website inquiries general. Do not submit detailed medical records, medication lists, Social Security numbers, Medicaid or Medicare numbers, diagnosis details, or other sensitive personal information through the public form.

Why our home feels different
