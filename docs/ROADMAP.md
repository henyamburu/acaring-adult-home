# Roadmap — ACaring Adult Home Website

_Last updated: 2026-06-09_

## Roadmap philosophy

Move from **basic presence** → **contact/referral funnel** → **trust-building local resource**.

Do not overbuild before the site proves that people are visiting and inquiring.

---

## Phase 0 — Stabilize current website

**Goal:** Make sure the current static site is complete, clean, and deployable.

### Tasks

- Confirm every page exists and loads.
- Confirm navigation is consistent on all pages.
- Remove broken links and placeholder content.
- Confirm location information is accurate.
- Confirm phone number, email, and business name are accurate.
- Confirm images exist and are privacy-safe.
- Confirm Cloudflare Pages deployment is functioning.
- Confirm analytics script is installed and not duplicated.

### Exit criteria

- Site can be visited without obvious broken elements.
- All top-level pages are reachable.
- Contact page has clear next action.

---

## Phase 1 — Contact and inquiry workflow

**Goal:** Make it easy for families and referral partners to start a conversation without collecting unnecessary sensitive health data.

### Recommended form approach

Use a minimal inquiry form that collects only what is needed to respond:

- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Relationship to prospective resident
- Preferred contact method
- General inquiry type
- Optional short message with instruction not to include sensitive medical details

### Avoid collecting

- Diagnosis details
- Medication lists
- Social Security numbers
- Medicaid/Medicare numbers
- Full date of birth
- Detailed health history
- Behavioral incident history
- Legal/guardianship records

### Exit criteria

- Test submission reaches the intended inbox.
- Confirmation message appears after submission.
- Form has spam protection.
- Form copy clearly says not to submit sensitive medical information.

---

## Phase 2 — Referral partner readiness

**Goal:** Make the site useful for professionals who may refer residents.

### Tasks

- Improve `referrals.html` with a clear referral path.
- Add “Who to contact” section.
- Add “Information we can discuss by phone” section.
- Add referral partner CTA buttons.
- Add downloadable or printable referral summary later if needed.

### Possible future page

- `placement-process.html`
- `family-guide.html`
- `referral-partners.html`

### Exit criteria

- Referral partners can understand fit quickly.
- Site supports direct outreach to case managers, discharge planners, WA Cares network contacts, and placement coordinators.

---

## Phase 3 — Local visibility bridge

**Goal:** Connect the inquiry workflow to trust-building content by making the site easier for local families and referral partners to find and understand.

### Tasks

- Keep page titles and meta descriptions unique and natural.
- Maintain consistent ACaring Adult Home NAP details in the footer.
- Keep LocalBusiness structured data aligned with verified public contact details.
- Maintain sitemap and robots files.
- Use privacy-safe FAQ and referral content to answer common Federal Way Adult Family Home search questions.
- Track optional inquiry source without making the form feel like medical intake.

### Exit criteria

- Search users can find clear Home, Care Services, Who We Serve, Referrals, FAQ, and Contact paths.
- Local wording is factual and not keyword-stuffed.
- No public form asks for sensitive medical or identity information.

---

## Phase 4 — Events and activities

**Goal:** Make `events.html` useful without becoming a high-maintenance calendar.

### Suggested structure

- Upcoming activities
- Recent moments from daily life
- Community involvement
- Family visit reminders or seasonal notes
- Monthly activity theme

### Keep it simple

This does not need to be a full event management system yet. A static card layout is enough for Phase 3.

### Exit criteria

- Events page has current content.
- Old events are removed or moved to “Past activities.”
- Images used are privacy-safe.

---

## Phase 5 — Trust and local authority content

**Goal:** Build a light content hub that answers family and referral questions.

### Possible guide topics

- How to choose an Adult Family Home in Federal Way
- Adult Family Home vs assisted living
- Questions families should ask before placement
- What to expect during a care home visit
- How referral partners can start a placement conversation
- WA Cares Fund provider pathway overview, if applicable and verified

### Exit criteria

- Site becomes useful even before a family contacts the home.
- Pages support outreach and credibility.

---

## Phase 6 — Light data-backed system

**Goal:** Use the website as part of an operating system for inquiries and growth.

### Possible additions

- Inquiry tracking spreadsheet or CRM
- Source tracking: website, case manager, WA Cares, hospital, referral partner
- Form-to-email and form-to-sheet workflow
- Monthly traffic and inquiry review
- Referral partner outreach log
- Content calendar

### Exit criteria

- Website can support measurable resident acquisition work.
- Inquiry sources are tracked.
- Follow-ups are not lost.
