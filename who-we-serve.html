Who we serve

A good fit for adults who need

  • A smaller home setting instead of a large facility
  • Daily structure, reminders, and supervision for safety
  • Support related to developmental disabilities
  • Dementia-related or aging-related care needs
  • Personal care and medication assistance
  • Consistent routines and a calm, respectful environment
Referrals welcome from

Placement partners

  • Families and guardians
  • Case managers and representatives
  • Hospital discharge planners
  • Placement coordinators
  • Community referral partners
