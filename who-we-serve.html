Who We Serve

Understanding care fit before the first visit.

ACaring Adult Home welcomes general inquiries for adults who may benefit from daily support in a smaller Federal Way Adult Family Home setting. Fit is always reviewed individually.

Referral Information Contact Us

A good fit may include adults who need

  • A smaller residential home setting
  • Help with personal care and daily routines
  • Meals, laundry, and household support
  • Medication reminders or assistance according to the care plan
  • Daily supervision, structure, and respectful communication

Fit depends on assessment

Placement decisions depend on resident assessment, care needs, safety considerations, licensing requirements, staffing, payer requirements, current availability, and whether the home can safely meet the resident's needs.

Families and referral partners can start with a general call, email, or inquiry form submission.
