Care Services

Adult Family Home services in Federal Way.

ACaring Adult Home provides general daily support in a smaller residential setting. Services depend on each resident's assessment, care plan, licensing requirements, staffing, and whether the home can safely meet the resident's needs.

Who We Serve Ask About Availability

Personal care support

Help with routines such as bathing, dressing, grooming, hygiene, and daily reminders based on the resident's care plan.

Meals and household routines

Home-style meals, snacks, laundry, housekeeping, and practical daily support in a residential environment.

Supervision and structure

Daily oversight, reminders, communication, and routines that support comfort, dignity, and safety.

Care is reviewed individually

This page is a general overview, not a placement guarantee or medical assessment. A direct conversation helps determine whether the home may be an appropriate fit.
